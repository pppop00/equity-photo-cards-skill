# Design specification — five-card schema v5

## Canvas and identity

- Logical frame: 1080×1350.
- Default export: 2160×2700.
- One confirmed palette across all five cards.
- Shared header/footer/date and Chinese short name.
- Official transparent logo on Card 1; no screenshot or ticker-letter substitute.

## Layouts

- Card 1: title/intro, logo/identity, headline metric row, then three aligned one-minute sections. Business model and risk may use two lines; the two core variables occupy separate one-line beats.
- Card 2: industry structure, an ordered external-condition → transmission → company-outcome → watch-signal chain, then Porter visualization and evidence. The visual score never replaces the written transmission.
- Card 3: upper five-year causal story and dated inflections; lower 2×3 financial grid ordered profitability, cash flow, leverage.
- Card 4: 2×2 quality panels. Each shows finding, evidence, and watch item. Valuation may add 1–2 concise as-of metrics. Unknown line at bottom. No aggregate score.
- Card 5: geography strip, 2×3 country panels, warning/insight/unknown footer. Each panel shows country fact, arrowed company transmission, and observable metric.

## Copy hierarchy

Attribution phrases are visible but compact. Full citations, formulas, and confidence metadata stay outside the card. A `basis_label` appears only where definitions are easy to confuse. Unknown or not-comparable content must remain legible and cannot be removed for visual neatness.

The deterministic renderer's line and pixel checks are authoritative. Do not solve overflow by reducing body text below the configured minimum font; return overfull copy to the writer.
