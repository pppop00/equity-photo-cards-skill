# Card 6 Logic: 金融豹判断逻辑

Use this reference before writing or rewriting Card 6 (`post_title`, `post_content_lines`, `hashtags`). It defines the default **reasoning logic** for the social post image. Do not force every output to sound identical to the examples.

## Logic Target

Think like a financially literate founder explaining one company to a sharp friend:

- dense but readable Chinese
- one hard financial fact, then the real business mechanism behind it
- optional metaphors from toll booths, printing presses, moats, chessboards, city walls, defense lines, engines, and surgery, used only when they clarify the business mechanism
- explicit contrast when useful: `表面是 X，本质是 Y` / `不是 X，而是 Y` / `对比 A，B 更像 C`
- judgment with edge, but no internet slang or rage-bait
- every sentence should teach the reader how to read the company, not merely summarize the report

The best Card 6 usually has this reasoning order:

1. identity or hard fact
2. hidden contradiction
3. metaphor or comparison
4. forward variable that can prove or break the valuation story

Before writing, build a compact analysis pack from the report package:

- **Competitors:** named peers, substitutes, price war / product competition, and what they reveal about the company's pricing power or moat.
- **Five-year change:** how the company has changed over roughly the past five years: revenue mix, margin structure, business model, geography, technology stack, capital intensity, regulation, or customer base.
- **Forward outlook:** the next one to two years' most important validation variables: margin, backlog conversion, bad-debt rate, delivery cost, ACV, retention, utilization, cash flow, pricing power, capacity, or policy exposure.
- **Geography:** where the company operates and where revenue comes from; separate operating footprint from revenue exposure when the report provides both.

## Structural Preference

For this renderer, still output **exactly four** `post_content_lines` as three statements plus one question. Make the four lines follow the same logic as the examples, not the same surface wording:

- line 1: company identity + one defining financial fact, optionally including revenue geography if it changes the story
- line 2: five-year transformation or business-model interpretation, with a metaphor only if useful
- line 3: competitor / industry / recent-context comparison that changes how the report should be read
- line 4: one pointed forward-looking question about the variable that will prove or break the story

Do not start every line with the same teaching marker. Sentence engines below are optional tools, not templates:

- `{公司}靠...撑起...，但...`
- `表面看...，本质上...`
- `对比{竞品/同行}，{公司}更像...`
- `剥开{叙事/外壳}，真正的...是...`
- `这不是...，而是...`
- `当...开始...，估值逻辑就在...`
- `真正要验证的是，...能不能...？`

## Positive Examples

These examples define the target reasoning logic. Reuse the diagnostic moves, not the wording, metaphors, or company facts.

### ServiceNow

Title: `一天吃透一家公司：ServiceNow`

- `ServiceNow由98%续约率筑起企业数字化路税大户，132.8亿美元营收和54.4亿美元经营现金流很漂亮，但剥开订阅收入外壳，本质上是企业内部流程碎片化带来的过路费。`
- `277亿美元RPO像定海神针，但在AI工作流重构的今天，旧时代繁文缛节的垄断更像一座修在旧高速路上的豪华收费站。`
- `对比Salesforce的Agentforce和微软Copilot的渗透，NOW花77亿美元买Armis不是讲新故事，而是在安全TAM前提前撒防御性筹码。`
- `真正要验证的是，Now Assist带来的ACV扩张能不能顶住cRPO放缓和同质化竞争？`

## Avoid

- Do not write a four-line template that merely swaps company names and numbers.
- Do not force the same metaphors, sentence openings, or paragraph rhythm across unrelated companies.
- Do not overuse `财报里`、`换句话说`、`结合最近`、`真要看`.
- Do not end with empty stock-market questions such as `未来还能涨吗？`
- Do not use forum hype: `这波`、`离谱`、`吃瓜`、`笑死`、`好家伙`、`破防`、`扎心`、`爆款`、`杀疯了`.
- Do not paste news headlines; explain why the event changes profit, pricing power, cash flow, or valuation.

## Rewrite Test

Before accepting Card 6, check:

- Can at least two lines survive without the company name and still show the same diagnostic logic rather than a sell-side summary?
- If there is a metaphor, does it clarify the business model instead of decorating it?
- Is the final question about a measurable variable: margin, backlog conversion, bad-debt rate, delivery cost, ACV, retention, utilization, cash flow, or pricing power?
- If the line starts with a fixed marker, remove the marker and see whether the sentence still has force. If not, rewrite the sentence.
